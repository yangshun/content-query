## Invite your team

Open **Workspace settings → Members** and choose **Invite member**. Enter an email address and select a role before sending the invitation.

- **Editors** can create projects, write notes, and leave comments.
- **Readers** can view projects and leave comments.
- **Owners** also manage members and workspace settings.

Start people as readers when they only need to follow along. An owner can change their role at any time.

## Share a single note

For someone outside your workspace, open a note and choose **Share → Create read-only link**. Only that note is visible through the link; the rest of the project stays private.

Anyone with the link can read the note. Avoid including private material, and turn the link off from the same menu when it is no longer needed.

## Keep the conversation close

Highlight a passage to add a comment. Use comments for questions and feedback, then put the outcome back into the note so the next reader has the full picture.

Before inviting a larger group, make sure your [projects are easy to navigate](/docs/organizing-projects).
